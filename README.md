## 🎮 About the Game

Fell & Sell is a single-player action RPG and shop-management game developed by Art Games Studio S.A. It released on August 26, 2026. Combining first-person mod-and-slash roguelike dungeon runs with fantasy retail simulation, the game challenges players to gather loot, craft equipment, manage a shop, and grow their merchant business.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Fell &amp; Sell**. This trainer enhances the core dungeon-crawling, crafting, inventory, combat, and shop-management systems in Fell & Sell. It provides optional control over player survivability, movement, resources, loot, crafting supplies, and shop funds while preserving the game's central loop of exploring dangerous areas, collecting goods, and reinvesting profits.

**Target Audience:** Suitable for single-player users who want to explore dungeons with fewer risks, experiment with crafting and shop layouts, accelerate business progression, or focus on the game's combat and merchant-management systems.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Movement takes place from a first-person perspective as you travel between the shop, dungeon areas, and the local forest. Use the movement option when you want to cover ground quickly, retreat from enemies, or speed up resource-gathering trips. Disable it before precise interactions with objects, shop fixtures, or quest locations.

### Tools & Inventory

Dungeon expeditions provide the loot used for crafting and retail stock. Keep the inventory options disabled while collecting new items, then activate the quantity or loot features after confirming that the desired item is selected. Materials can be used for crafting, while finished equipment and other goods can be placed on shop shelves for customers.

### World & Environment

The main gameplay cycle connects the shop with descending roguelike dungeons and the nearby forest. Explore for resources, return safely with your haul, complete objectives through the Quest Board, and reinvest profits into fixtures, decorations, upgrades, and additional merchandise. Shop decorations can also provide combat benefits for future expeditions.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is built around fast first-person mod-and-slash encounters. Use health protection for safer dungeon runs and damage options when dealing with groups of enemies or powerful bosses. Collect rewards after each successful expedition and return to the shop before taking another risk, since survival determines how much loot can be converted into profit.
